# FoodLoop

FoodLoop helps good food find people who need it.

Restaurants, community members, and other food providers can share surplus food with local nonprofits and people in need. Recipients can discover available food nearby and arrange to claim it, helping communities make better use of food that might otherwise go to waste.

## How it works

- Food providers list surplus food and share details such as quantity, availability, and pickup information.
- Nonprofits and other recipients browse available offers and request food they can use.
- Providers and recipients coordinate pickup so the food reaches the community in time.

## Our purpose

FoodLoop aims to reduce food waste and make surplus food easier to share. By connecting local providers with people and organizations that can use it, every listing can help turn extra food into a meaningful contribution.

## Get involved

Whether you have food to share or are looking for support, FoodLoop brings your community together around a simple idea: **share good food, reduce waste, and help more people.**
